Step 2: Industry Research

Evaluate market trends, competitive dynamics, and growth opportunities.

Step 3: Valuation

Perform DCF analysis, comparable companies analysis, and precedent transaction analysis.

Step 4: Investment Recommendation

Develop a professional investment opinion supported by financial and strategic analysis.
